A collection of the main game interfaces, showcasing the home screen, arrow puzzles, outros, quests and collections, daily challenges, monetization, and other features.

Game Flow & Navigation

The game flow introduces players to Ace Maze through a branded loading screen, followed by a home screen that brings level progression, rewards, and daily challenges into one place. The interface highlights the current level and difficulty, tracks progress toward milestones, and provides quick access to missions, VIP rewards, and settings. A consistent visual hierarchy, clear navigation, and prominent primary actions help players understand their progress and jump into gameplay with minimal friction.

Outro

The outro celebrates puzzle completion with a “Perfect!” message and a detailed score breakdown showing precision bonus, clear points, and streak bonus. A mini leaderboard displays top player rankings, scores, and reward tiers, adding a competitive element that motivates players to improve their performance and aim for higher ranks.

Daily Quest

The Daily Quest section encourages regular gameplay through goal-based tasks. The My Journey feature adds a visual progression system where a flower character grows through milestones, making progress more engaging and rewarding.

Achievement

The achievement system lets players track their accomplishments through collectible badges and visible progress. It creates a sense of growth and long-term motivation, encouraging players to continue playing and work toward new milestones.

Feature Flow & Navigation

The Daily Challenge turns consistency into a rewarding ritual, with a calendar that celebrates completed puzzles and milestone rewards that keep players coming back. Each day brings a timed arrow puzzle where speed earns stars, adding a competitive spark and encouraging players to beat their best performance. Together, daily progress and star ratings make every challenge feel like another step toward mastery.
